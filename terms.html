Portal access and appropriate use

Use of this portal

Access is intended for authorized hospital personnel and permitted users. Use only your assigned account and access only information needed for your role.

Do not disclose credentials, export patient data unnecessarily, or use this portal for unauthorized purposes. Follow hospital procedures for patient information and account security.

Browser-side role checks improve usability but do not replace backend authorization. Availability and functionality may depend on network and backend service status.
