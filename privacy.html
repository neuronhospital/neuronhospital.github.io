How this browser portal handles information

Privacy and browser storage

This portal processes appointment and operational information to support hospital workflows. Information submitted through forms is sent to the configured hospital backend.

Login credentials are retained in tab-scoped browser session storage for the authenticated session. Some preferences and operational caches may be stored locally in this browser.

Do not use a shared or public device for patient workflows. Log out when finished. Browser storage can be cleared from browser settings.

This page is a concise portal notice, not a substitute for the hospital's formally approved privacy policy. Contact the hospital for policy details or data-related requests.
